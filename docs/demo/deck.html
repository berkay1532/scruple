Problem

The fastest-growing customers can't be billed.

  • AI APIs earn per request but can't bill per request — 2.9% + $0.30 card minimums make a $0.001 charge impossible, so everyone falls back to prepaid credit walls.
  • AI agents hold no payment instrument — no card, no limits, no accountability.
  • Crypto has no pull payments — and its approval UX is "unlimited allowance, trust me."
Product

One platform, two faces.

BILLING — the merchant side

Metered: one middleware line turns any endpoint into HTTP-402 pay-per-call, settled in batches via Circle Gateway — $0.0003 payments become economical.

Recurring: on-chain plans with immutable versioned pricing, permissionless charging, 3-day grace — pull payments crypto never had.

CARDS — the payer side

Mint a card: periodic budget, merchant allowlist, expiry — then hand the signer key to a human or an AI agent. Freeze instantly, cancel anytime. No funds ever parked on the card.

A corporate card for software.

Non-custodial end-to-end: money moves wallet-to-wallet; the 1% fee is an on-chain split at settlement.
